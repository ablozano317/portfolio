HoneyHive

Role: Developer & Designer

An awareness website that shares information about declining bee populations and encourages support for bee conservation.

Project overview

HoneyHive is a web page focused on sharing information and raising awareness about declining bee populations. As a part of a College project, it aimed to educate the public and encourage action to support bee conservation efforts.

Ideation

HoneyHive was conceived as an approachable way to introduce visitors to the issue of declining bee populations. The bee-inspired identity supports the subject matter and gives the awareness page a clear, memorable message. Visually we wanted our page to function like a pamphlet, guiding visitors through the information in a structured and engaging manner.

Challenges

The main challenge was presenting an important environmental issue in a way that feels inviting and easy to understand. The page needed to balance its visual identity with clear information about why bee populations matter.

Results

HoneyHive resulted in a published awareness website that gives visitors a focused place to learn about declining bee populations.
