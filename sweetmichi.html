Sweet Michi

Role: Developer & Designer

A cohesive, welcoming website for a home-baked goods business, designed to showcase products and support customer orders.

Project overview

Sweet Michi started off as my baking side hustle project built on the premise of cat shaped treats back in 2022. "Michi" is a diminutive term for a cat in spanish so it felt fitting to name the business. The goal was to create a website that would allow me to showcase my products and facilitate customer ordering experience. The website was built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a focus on creating a visually appealing and user-friendly experience for visitors.

Ideation

It was important that this website reflected sweetness associated with home baked goods. I took inspiration from bakery websites and cafes primarily those that focused on overly sweet aesthetics and pastel color palettes. I researched into japanese sweet lolita cafes, taking the inspitation to incorprate lace motifs throughout the page. To add a sense of playfulness, I also took inspiration from cat cafes and the use of cat imagery in branding and marketing. Additionally the use of handwritten fonts and illustrations was inteded to create a sense of familiarty and homeyness. Overall, we needed a balance of fancy, sweet yet approachable and playful (like a cat).

Challenges

- Balance the need for a cohesive brand identity with the practical requirements of showcasing products and facilitating customer ordering.
- Implement the necessary features for customers to place orders while not risking security or usability.

Feedback

Customers have provided positive feedback on the website's design and functionality, with many noting that it is easy to navigate and visually appealing. The website has also received praise for its cohesive branding and attention to detail. However, there are critiques regarding a complex ordering process which canbe improved with more advanced backend functionality. Overall, the feedback has been positive and has helped to inform future updates and improvements to the website..

Results

The website gives Sweet Michi a cohesive online presence to showcase its baked goods and support customer orders. Feedback highlighted its appealing design and easy navigation, while also identifying the ordering flow as an opportunity for future improvement.
