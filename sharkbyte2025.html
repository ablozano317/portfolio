SharkByte

Role: UX Designer

Creating a website for SharkByte, MDC's premiere hackathon.

Project overview

As a part of an initiative of INIT MDC North to host Miami Dade College's first signature collegiate hackathon, a website was needed. Our objective was to create a website that promotes the event, maintains a strong visual identity constent with campus branding, and dne in a timely manner. The website needed to be both engaging and functional, ensuring that potential participants could easily access event details and feel excited about participating.

Ideation

The SharkByte name and logo provided the starting point for the website's visual direction. I developed supporting imagery that carried the shark and technology theme through the experience. Our main visual inspiration consisted of pixel-art style graphics and vibrant colors that encompassed the spirit of Miami Dade's technology scene.

Challenges

- A key challenge was making the event information easy to follow while giving the site a distinctive identity. The imagery and user flow needed to work together so visitors could understand the event and find the details they needed.
- Pixel art requires a stylistic precision that made it difficult to create a seamless flow throughout the page.
- There were heavy time constraints as the page had to be ready by the start of the fall semester for timely registration.
- Ensuring the website was accessible and user-friendly for all potential participants.

Results

SharkByte 2025 welcomed more than 200 attendees in its inaugural year. The event was renewed at MDC for 2026, and the website's imagery and user flow helped present the event to participants.
