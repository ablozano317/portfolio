TrueFan

Role: UX Designer

A hackathon project that turns cinematic universes into interactive constellation maps, helping fans explore stories and viewing orders.

Project overview

TrueFan turns any franchise into an interactive constellation. See where to start, watch in order, unlock entries as you go, and ask AI about confusing lore. Never watch out of order again. This platform provides a unique and engaging way for fans to explore and interact with their favorite franchises, sharing their journey and discoveries with the community of fellow enthusiasts.The goal was to create an engaging and easier formatting to explore and log franchises. Among other features, the project included recommendations, social sharing platform, and a dynamic viewing order system.

Ideation

We used constellations as the visual metaphor for connecting entries in a franchise. This gave the map a distinctive identity while making each story feel like part of a larger universe.We wanted to engage users as if they were outerspace explorers navigating a vast universe of stories. We implemented features such as "unlocked constellations" that represented the progression of a user's journey through the franchise, making the experience both rewarding and immersive.

Challenges

- Completing a functional website in 36-hours.
- Release order, chronological order, and fan-recommended order often disagree. We had to decide which to recommend by default and how to show connected stories without implying that one order is universally correct.
- Franchise data is messy. Titles change between regions, OVAs and specials are listed inconsistently, and some relationships ("takes place between episodes 12 and 13") aren't captured in any database.
- Making a big universe readable. A franchise with 50+ entries turns into a hairball fast. We iterated on layout, clustering, and zoom levels until the galaxy stayed clear.

Results

TrueFan received positive feedback from users and judges from KnightHacks judging. In the future we plan to expand by Add more franchises, and let fans suggest and vote on viewing orders. Switch between release, chronological, and "essentials only" paths. Sync progress with watch-tracking services like AniList, Trakt, and Letterboxd. Show where to stream each entry. Build a conversational AI guide that explains lore and relationships using only information the user has unlocked. Expand beyond TV and film to games, manga, and books that share the same universe.
